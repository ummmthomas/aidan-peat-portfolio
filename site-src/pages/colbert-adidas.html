The Superstar, saved by a lobster: a classic sneaker turned into a Colbert piece people can wear.

adidas brought the Superstar, one of its most recognisable shoes. Philip Colbert brought his lobster and a message, "Save the Lobster". The shoe keeps its shell toe and three stripes and lets Colbert's world cover the rest.

The two brands

adidas

The Superstar is a sports shoe that became street culture: white leather, three stripes, the rubber shell toe. Its audience already treats it as a canvas. [[FACT: the audience this release was aimed at]]

Philip Colbert

A London-based pop artist with a global following for his cartoon lobster persona, whose work moves easily between gallery, toy shelf and street.

The brand bridge

The silhouette stays pure adidas: white upper, black stripes, Superstar branding on the side. Colbert arrives as a repeat pattern of hand-lettered "Save the Lobster" slogans across the quarters, with the red lobster breaking out at the heel. From across a room it's a Superstar. Up close it's a Colbert.
