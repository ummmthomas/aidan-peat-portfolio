The two brands

ASUS

A Taipei-based technology brand. The Vivobook 13 Slate OLED is a detachable Windows laptop that comes with a pen. [[FACT: the audience this edition was aimed at]]

Philip Colbert

A London-based pop artist with a global following for his cartoon lobster persona and its world of eggs, eyes and lightning bolts.

The brand bridge

ASUS released the Colbert edition as one of two limited artist editions, so the job was to make a laptop feel worth collecting. Everything around the device carries Colbert's world: a sticker-bombed box, a "COLBERT" sleeve, a lobster figure that holds the stylus and red shoelaces. The laptop itself stays a working ASUS machine, with Colbert's artwork on screen.
