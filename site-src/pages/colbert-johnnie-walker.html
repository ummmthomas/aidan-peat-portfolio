Scotch heritage meets Mr. Lobster: limited gift boxes made to be collected.

Johnnie Walker brought its top-shelf Blue Label and XR21 whiskies. Philip Colbert brought Mr. Lobster, the cartoon alter ego at the centre of his pop world. The result is a pair of gift sets that look like Johnnie Walker and feel like a Colbert edition.

The two brands

Johnnie Walker

Scotch whisky with more than two centuries of blending behind it. Blue Label and XR21 sit at the top of the range: bottles people buy to give, and to mark an occasion. [[FACT: the audience this edition was aimed at]]

Philip Colbert

A London-based pop artist often called the "godson of Andy Warhol", with a global following for his cartoon lobster persona. His work crosses old-master and art-history themes with everyday symbols of mass culture.

The brand bridge

Each box keeps what makes the whisky recognisable and lets Mr. Lobster take over the rest. Blue Label stays in its deep blue, with Mr. Lobster sitting on a full moon among clouds. XR21's warm gold is turned into a field of botanicals around a crowned lobster.

The set goes beyond the box: a glass carries both names, and it sits on a gold lobster-shaped stand that makes the pour part of the artwork. For a whisky buyer it's a gift that stands out on the shelf. For a Colbert collector it's an edition worth keeping.
