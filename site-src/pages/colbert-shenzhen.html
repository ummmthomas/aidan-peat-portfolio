Colbert's first show in South China, announced across the whole building.

Philip Colbert: The Myth of the Lobster Planet filled the Sea World Culture and Arts Center in Shekou with more than 40 works. Before anyone got inside, the building itself told the city the show was on.

The graphic system

The façade carries the show at street scale: a full-height banner pairing Colbert's octopus-lobster sculpture with a bilingual title lockup, 龙虾创世 and The Myth of the Lobster Planet, and the dates set large enough to read from across the road. Vertical flags repeat the Chinese title.
